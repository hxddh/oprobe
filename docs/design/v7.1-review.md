# oprobe 7.1 review: bugs, product power, and an app that shows everything

Status: plan. Date: 2026-11-24. Basis: 7.0.0 (`0a177c5`).

This review has two sources:
- **A bug hunt.** Every lab scenario was run through `run`, `show` (all views) and `prove`, along with multiple operators, budgets, Ctrl-C, corrupt files and a real `oprobe-trace` file.
- **A screenshot pass over the app.** It was the GTK build, under Xvfb. It covered Profile, Operator and Compare on the demo, throttle, flaky, parquet and bandwidth runs.

Every item below was reproduced. Nothing here adds a command, a flag or a concept. 7.1 is additive.

## What already holds

- **Contracts:** all JSON validates against `schemas/`, and corrupt files fail cleanly.
- **Ranges:** the range numbers match the full run.
- **Top findings:** they match the injected fault for demo, throttle, stragglers, ratelimit, parquet, hotkey and timevarying.
- **Proves:** they improve demo (+30 %), stragglers (+92 %) and parquet (+49 %).

## 2. The app shows everything, honestly

The screenshot pass found these, in order of what misleads most.

1. **The timeline runs past the window.** A drain interval after 8 s or 10 s drops goodput to zero on every chart, and in Compare. Clip the charts to the measured window, as the tiles are.
2. **Retries, throttled and failed series are invisible.** They share an axis with requests per second (1 000s), so they are flat at zero. Give "Problems per second" (retries, throttled responses, failed operations) its own small chart, drawn only when any are non-zero.
3. **No throughput over time.** Bandwidth is a headline tile but has no timeline. Add MiB/s per interval (read and written).
4. **The latency distribution hides the tail.** A linear 0–100 % CDF is flat above p90, which is where every finding lives. Draw it as "share slower than" on a log scale (a tail plot), with p50, p99 and p99.9 marked. Do the same for the evidence chart of `tail_stragglers`.
5. **The heatmap is coarse.** Stragglers at 260 ms paint a band from 150 ms to 1 s, although every row was kept. Use the fine rows (`HeatmapView`), as 6.3 meant.
6. **Finding evidence prints raw keys** ("Affected apis", "P50", "Typical ttfb", "261ms"). Give them labels and units from the catalog, in the same format as the tiles.
7. **Details are tables only.** Hot keys show "—" for errors and p99, size buckets read "0" and "<1MiB", and "cold/warm" means first read versus re-read, not connections.
   - Rename "cold/warm" to "first read / re-read".
   - Label the size buckets as ranges.
   - Fill in or drop empty columns.
   - Add an inline share bar to the share columns.
8. **Noise.** Drop "Writes: part size … no multipart parts" when nothing was written in parts. Drop "HTTP/2 0". Hide a chart legend entry whose series is all zero.
9. **Compare is thin.** Add:
   - p99.9, requests/s, retries, failed operations and throttled responses to the table;
   - overlays of latency over time and requests per second;
   - a per-API diff (requests, p99 TTFB).
10. **Requests by API** gets an inline p50/p99 bar per API, so the slow API stands out without reading numbers.
